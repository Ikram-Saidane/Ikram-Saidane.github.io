Breast cancer detection: 6 models head to head

116 patients, 9 biomarkers (glucose, insulin, HOMA, resistin…). We compared logistic regression, Ridge, Lasso, Naive Bayes, K-NN and an MLP, each tuned with GridSearchCV on 5 × 5 repeated stratified cross-validation.

In medicine, a missed cancer costs more than a false alarm. So we judged the models on sensitivity and specificity, not just accuracy, and then analysed the decision threshold.
